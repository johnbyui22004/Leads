Our Story

A Family Business Built on Trust

Sterling Auto Glass started in 2001 in Bill Sterling's home garage, fixing windshields for neighbors and friends who didn't want to drive across town to a big chain. Word got around fast, and within a few years Bill and his wife Carol opened their first small shop on Main Street.

Today, their son Mark runs day-to-day operations, but the values haven't changed: show up on time, do the job right the first time, and treat every customer's car like it's our own. Most of our technicians have been with us for over a decade, and many of our customers have been coming back for twenty years.

We're not a national chain answering to a call center. We're your neighbors — and we'll be here for your next windshield, too.
